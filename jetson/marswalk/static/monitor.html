Shows what the app draws, from the wearer's head: HUD, map, menus, pointer, arrows, obstacle outline, plus a 1 m floor grid and dots on the hand joints. The room is behind it when the headset's browser gave the app a camera. If the grid does not lie flat on the real floor, set the camera lens at the top right: field of view until the 1 m squares fit the floor near and far, tilt until the grid sits on the floor. Whole view shows all the wearer sees, Camera fills the frame with the camera picture. The hub keeps what was set. F shows the picture alone.
